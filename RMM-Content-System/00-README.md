# Resume Money Method — Content System

This repository is the operating system for Resume Money Method, also called RMM.

RMM is a $20 digital guide that teaches beginners how to make money offering AI-assisted resume cleanup services.

This is not career advice for job seekers. It is a simple service business system for beginner side hustlers who want extra income without learning a complicated online business.

## Main goal

The content system should move viewers through this funnel:

comments → DMs → free checklist downloads → $20 guide sales

## Core content format

Every TikTok post is a 5-slide slideshow.

The most important performance rule:

Slide 1 creates curiosity.
Slide 2 rewards the curiosity by clearly explaining the mechanism or opportunity.

Slide 2 should never be vague. It should make the offer clear fast.

## Brand language

Use:

- resume cleanup service
- AI-assisted resume cleanup
- simple service business
- beginner service business
- extra income idea
- make money helping people with resumes

Avoid:

- passive income
- escape the 9-5
- get rich quick
- income guarantees
- professional resume writing
- resume rewrite business
- guru language
- hype language
