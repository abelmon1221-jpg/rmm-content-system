# Performance Tracking

The goal is not just views.

The goal is:

comments → DMs → free checklist downloads → $20 guide sales

---

## Metrics to track

Track these for every post:

- views
- Slide 1 to Slide 2 drop-off
- swipe-through to Slide 5
- comments
- RESUME comments
- DMs
- checklist clicks
- guide sales

---

## Main bottleneck

The biggest known bottleneck is Slide 1 to Slide 2 drop-off.

A high drop-off usually means Slide 2 did not reward the curiosity fast enough.

---

## Target improvement

**Current issue:**

Many posts lose around 70% of viewers from Slide 1 to Slide 2.

**First target:**

Reduce Slide 1 to Slide 2 drop-off to around 50%.

**Next target:**

Move toward 40%.

---

## Diagnostic patterns

### High views but low comments

The hook may be interesting, but the offer or CTA is not clear enough.

**Fix:**

Make the service clearer by Slide 2 and make the CTA more exchange-based.

---

### High Slide 1 views but bad Slide 2 retention

Slide 2 is probably too vague.

**Fix:**

Use a clear mechanism line:

> "It's a resume cleanup service."

or

> "People pay to have resumes cleaned up before they apply."

---

### Good swipe-through but no RESUME comments

The post may be useful, but the CTA may not feel worth commenting for.

**Fix:**

Make the checklist feel more concrete.

Example:

> "Comment RESUME and I'll send the starter checklist with the first steps."

---

### Good comments but low guide sales

The lead magnet may not be doing enough selling.

**Fix:**

Improve the checklist follow-up DM and landing page.
