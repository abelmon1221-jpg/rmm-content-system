# 5-Slide Slideshow Structure

Every RMM TikTok post should use this structure.

---

## Slide 1 — Curiosity hook

**Goal:**

Stop the scroll and create a reason to swipe.

Slide 1 should be specific, clear, and curiosity-driven.

It should create a small open loop.

**Good examples:**

- Most AI side hustles are too complicated for beginners.
- A resume cleanup service is easier to explain than most online businesses.
- If you want extra income, start with a service people already understand.
- People overthink AI side hustles. This one is simple.

---

## Slide 2 — Reward slide / mechanism

**Goal:**

Reward the curiosity immediately.

This is where the offer becomes clear.

Slide 2 should explain what the viewer is actually looking at.

**Good examples:**

- It's a resume cleanup service.
- People already pay to have their resumes cleaned up before they apply.
- AI helps fix the wording, bullets, formatting, and keywords.
- You are not writing from scratch. You are improving what they already have.

---

## Slide 3 — Beginner simplicity

**Goal:**

Make the viewer believe this is doable.

Break the biggest objection.

**Good examples:**

- You do not need to be a career coach.
- You need a process, three AI tools, and a clean delivery format.
- The client brings the resume. You clean it up.
- That makes it easier to start than most digital service ideas.

---

## Slide 4 — Tangible value

**Goal:**

Give one real detail that proves there is substance behind the method.

**Examples:**

- Start by fixing weak bullet points.
- Look for vague lines like "helped customers" and make them specific.
- Use AI to find better job-market keywords.
- Clean formatting matters because messy resumes get ignored fast.

---

## Slide 5 — Soft CTA

**Goal:**

Move the viewer to comment or DM.

**Good examples:**

- Want the free starter checklist? Comment RESUME.
- Comment RESUME and I'll send the simple checklist.
- Want the beginner version? Comment RESUME.
- Comment RESUME and I'll send the cleanup checklist.
