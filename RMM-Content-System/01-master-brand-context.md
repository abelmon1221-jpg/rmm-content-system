# Master Brand Context

## What RMM is

Resume Money Method is a $20 digital guide teaching beginners how to make money offering AI-assisted resume cleanup services.

The offer is not "become a professional resume writer."

The offer is simpler:

Help people clean up their resumes using AI.

That means improving wording, bullets, formatting, clarity, and job-market keywords with the help of AI tools.

## Who it is for

RMM is for beginner side hustlers, especially practical and skeptical adults ages 25–54 who want extra income without learning a complicated online business.

They may have tried or looked into things like dropshipping, affiliate marketing, print on demand, trading, YouTube automation, AI side hustles, or other online income methods.

They are skeptical because they have seen too much hype.

They do not want a complicated brand.

They do not want to become influencers.

They want something simple, understandable, and service-based.

## Who it is not for

RMM is not mainly for job seekers trying to fix their own resume.

It is not career coaching.

It is not employment advice.

It is not a professional resume writing certification.

It is a money method built around a simple service business.

## The core offer

The core offer is a resume cleanup service.

The buyer learns how to:

- use AI to improve resume wording
- clean up bullet points
- add stronger job-market keywords
- improve formatting and readability
- package the service simply
- find people who need resume help
- deliver the cleaned-up resume

## The tool stack

The method uses:

1. Grok for job-market and keyword research
2. ChatGPT for ATS-friendly resume structure and bullet cleanup
3. Claude for more natural, human-sounding wording and final polish

## Core positioning

RMM should feel discovered, not invented.

The voice should sound like:

"I found a simple service people understand, and AI makes it easier to deliver."

Not:

"I am a guru with a secret system."

## Core promise

A repeatable, low-overhead service someone can start learning with AI.

No income guarantees.

No passive income claims.

No fake lifestyle promise.

Just a clear process.

## Brand voice

The voice should be:

- direct
- practical
- mature
- calm
- skeptical-buyer aware
- plainspoken
- beginner-friendly

It should sound like a practical older brother explaining the idea, not a hype marketer selling a dream.
