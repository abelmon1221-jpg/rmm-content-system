# Canva Design Rules

## Format

Use vertical TikTok format:

**1080 x 1920**

Every post should have exactly 5 slides.

---

## Visual style

Use:

- dark or blurred backgrounds
- bold white text
- semi-transparent black rounded text boxes
- clean AI / money / resume aesthetic
- mature, simple design
- high contrast text

Avoid:

- bright guru-style colors
- fake luxury imagery
- too many emojis
- red arrows
- cluttered layouts
- small text
- stock photo cheese

---

## Slide design rules

Slide 1 should have the largest text.

Slide 1 text should sit in the upper third when possible so it survives TikTok feed cropping.

Each slide should contain one idea.

Keep text short enough to read in under two seconds.

Slide 5 should make "Comment RESUME" visually obvious.

---

## Brand feeling

The page should feel:

- practical
- simple
- mature
- direct
- trustworthy

It should not feel like a hype page.

---

## Canva prompt rule

When asking Canva or Claude to build slides, always include:

> Use a dark blurred background, bold white text, semi-transparent black rounded text boxes, and a clean AI / resume / money aesthetic. Keep each slide simple and easy to read on mobile.
