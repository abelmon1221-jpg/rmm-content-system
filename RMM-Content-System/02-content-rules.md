# RMM Content Rules

## Main content format

Every TikTok post must be a 5-slide slideshow.

No exceptions unless manually requested.

## Main funnel goal

Content should move viewers through:

comments → DMs → free checklist downloads → $20 guide sales

Views matter, but comments and buyer intent matter more.

## Slide 2 reward rule

This is one of the most important rules.

Slide 1 creates curiosity.

Slide 2 must reward that curiosity immediately by clearly explaining the mechanism or opportunity.

Do not make Slide 2 vague.

**Bad Slide 2 examples:**

- "Here's why"
- "Let me explain"
- "Most people miss this"
- "It's simpler than you think"

**Better Slide 2 examples:**

- "It's a resume cleanup service."
- "People pay to have their resumes cleaned up before they apply."
- "AI helps with the wording, bullets, and keywords."
- "You offer a simple service business around resume cleanup."
- "You are not writing from scratch. You are cleaning up what they already have."

## Offer language rules

Use:

- resume cleanup service
- AI-assisted resume cleanup
- simple service business
- beginner service business
- extra income idea
- make money helping people with resumes
- clean up resumes with AI

Avoid:

- professional resume writing
- resume rewrite business
- passive income
- escape the 9-5
- get rich quick
- guaranteed income
- easy money
- secret hack
- guru language
- hype language

## Audience rules

Speak to practical, skeptical adults.

The core audience is beginner side hustlers, especially adults 25–54 who want extra income without learning a complicated online business.

They need clarity, not hype.

They respond better to simple, mature explanations than loud money claims.

## Tone rules

The tone should be:

- calm
- direct
- practical
- mature
- honest
- skeptical-buyer aware

Avoid sounding like:

- a guru
- a teenager flexing
- a passive income influencer
- a fake expert
- a corporate coach

## Content goal by slide

Slide 1 should stop the scroll.

Slide 2 should explain the mechanism.

Slide 3 should make it feel beginner-friendly.

Slide 4 should give real value or a useful detail.

Slide 5 should ask for a low-pressure action.

## CTA rules

Primary CTA:

> Comment RESUME and I'll send the free starter checklist.

The CTA should feel like a clear exchange, not begging for engagement.

**Good CTA examples:**

- Comment RESUME and I'll send the free starter checklist.
- Want the free checklist? Comment RESUME.
- Comment RESUME and I'll send the simple version.

**Avoid:**

- Link in bio
- Buy now
- Don't miss out
- This changed my life
- Start making money today
